# News and FAQ Expansion V17

Date: July 31, 2026

## Outcome

- Sitemap: 440 to 525 canonical URLs.
- News: 35 to 70 independent `NewsArticle` URLs.
- FAQ: 100 to 150 independent FAQ URLs.
- English guides retained: 101.
- German details retained: 57.
- Japanese details retained: 12.
- Official language system retained: 15 languages.

## News coverage

Five new reports were added to each established topic:

1. Release Date
2. Guides and Systems
3. Game Database
4. Characters and Story
5. Platforms
6. FAQ Updates
7. Development and Sources

Each topic now has ten reports. Reports separate the dated fact, its practical
meaning, and the boundary of what the source does not yet confirm. The primary
evidence set is Remedy Entertainment, Steam, PlayStation Blog, and Xbox Wire.

## FAQ coverage

Fifty new answers were added across five player-intent clusters:

- release, store access, Mac timing, and cloud launch;
- combat resources, executions, The Gap, Artifacts, and New Game Plus;
- Dylan, Jesse, Zoe, The Board, Manhattan, and the Oldest House;
- Play Anywhere, PC requirements, controller support, cloud, and old consoles;
- language audio boundaries, source verification, and post-release updates.

Every FAQ has a unique slug, question, canonical URL, direct answer, explanatory
boundary, official source, relevant guide link, five related questions, and
`FAQPage` plus breadcrumb schema.

## Page-module coverage

Every English guide detail, every FAQ detail, every primary navigation
destination, and every landing/detail page reachable from the 15-language
dropdown now exposes:

- ten topic-matched News links;
- at least five related FAQ links;
- official evidence and relevant-guide paths.

## Validation

- 525 canonical URLs in `sitemap-0.xml`.
- 531 generated HTML files audited, including compatibility redirects and
  non-sitemap utility pages.
- 70 unique News slugs and titles.
- 150 unique FAQ slugs and questions.
- All News pages pass the 220-word visible-content gate.
- All FAQ pages pass the 180-word visible-content gate.
- Title, meta description, H1, canonical, schema, internal links, language
  dropdown, AdSense tags, and `ads.txt` passed the automated audit.
