## Quick answer

**Shield raises floating rocks for protection and can turn that defense into an offensive ram when Dylan dashes into enemies.** A Shield build therefore uses defense to create a safe melee approach rather than remaining stationary.

## Defensive and offensive jobs

Dylan's close-range focus means incoming threats do not stay neatly in front of him. Shield helps manage that approach problem, while the ram gives the ability a proactive endpoint. Once Dylan reaches the target, Aberrant hits replenish Combat Ability resource and can begin the stun-execution loop.

The build role differs from simple damage reduction. Positioning, target selection and the transition from guard to dash determine whether the ability creates momentum. Exact damage absorption, duration, direction coverage and upgrade nodes remain unpublished.

## Pre-launch loadout logic

Choose a weapon form that capitalizes on safe entry. Flurry can pressure quickly; Slash can address nearby groups; Slice targets a single enemy. Those are confirmed identities, not final tier rankings. A second Combat Ability can later add range or crowd control when Dylan's three slots become available.
