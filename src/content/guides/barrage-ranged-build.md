## Quick answer

**A Barrage-focused build uses the rock-projectile ability to solve range, then returns to Aberrant melee to refill its Combat Ability resource.** It is a hybrid plan, not a confirmed infinite-ranged build.

## Core role

Barrage pulls rocks from the ground and launches them at a target. Among the three first-boss choices shown to media, it is the most direct player-controlled ranged option. Shield emphasizes protection and a ram, while Seekers create autonomous pressure and can be thrown as explosives.

The strongest confirmed pairing principle is coverage. A close-range Primary Form can generate resource and handle isolated enemies. A wider Secondary Form or Combo Ender can protect Dylan when a ranged attack draws a group's attention. Final form modifiers and talent nodes are not published, so a specific six-piece loadout would be fictional.

## Combat sequence

Open or interrupt at range with Barrage, close the gap through movement, land melee attacks to recover resource, and use a stun opportunity for an execution. The execution's temporary melee buff turns the return to close combat into a payoff rather than a compromise.
