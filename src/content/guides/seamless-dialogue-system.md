## Quick answer

**Control Resonant introduces a seamless dialogue system that allows conversations to continue during exploration.** Remedy presents it as a way to keep players immersed while Zoe and other characters provide context.

## What “seamless” means here

The official combat-and-world deep dive says dialogue can unfold as Dylan moves through Manhattan. Zoe De Vera, an FBC field agent and Dylan's handler, is the clearest confirmed example: she guides him while events develop in the field.

This wording does not mean the game has no cutscenes, dialogue choices or pauses. It confirms an additional delivery method that does not always pull Dylan into a separate conversation screen.

## Narrative purpose

Dylan has spent years isolated inside the Oldest House, while Zoe has survived the crisis outside. Remedy describes their relationship around trust, responsibility and learning to rely on others. Letting them speak during exploration can connect the environment to that relationship without stopping movement every time new context is needed.

The system also supports larger zones. Field commentary can respond while architecture, encounters and discoveries remain visible, preserving the sense that the crisis is active around the player.

## What is not confirmed

There is no official dialogue-wheel specification, affinity system or proof that every conversation changes outcomes. World Quests and side stories may have decisions, but the seamless system itself should not be marketed as branching dialogue.

The final game is required to document subtitles, interruption behavior, replay logs and whether combat can cut a conversation short.
