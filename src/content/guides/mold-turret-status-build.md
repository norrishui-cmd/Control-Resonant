## Quick answer

**Mold Turrets enable a tactical summon build by attacking independently and applying harmful status effects.** Dylan can use that pressure to move, isolate targets and create melee or execution opportunities.

## What makes the build distinct

The official example contrasts a heavy close-range setup with a tactical approach using summons. A Mold Turret adds an actor to the battlefield rather than only producing one hit. Its status application also creates a potential connection to Talent passives built around effects on enemies.

That does not prove the final debuff name, stack limit, duration or boss effectiveness. It also does not prove that every turret reference in preview coverage points to the same upgrade or ability. The useful pre-release conclusion is its role: independent damage plus harmful status pressure.

## Tactical sequence

Deploy the turret where it can cover an encounter, then use Dylan's movement to prevent enemies from collapsing on one position. Select an Aberrant form based on the remaining threat: a wide form for groups or a focused form for a priority target. Melee hits refill the ability resource, allowing the summon plan to remain connected to active combat.
