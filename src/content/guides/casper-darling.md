## Quick answer

**Dr. Casper Darling is confirmed to appear in Control Resonant.** During the opening hands-on, he appears briefly in a full-motion-video clip describing the period when Dylan was first taken into the Federal Bureau of Control.

## What the preview establishes

The appearance confirms two things without requiring story speculation: Darling returns on screen, and Control Resonant continues the first game's use of live-action FBC recordings. His clip connects to Dylan's history as a Bureau captive, test subject and director candidate.

The preview does not establish whether Darling is physically present in 2026, communicating from another state, or seen only through archived material. His location and the number of scenes remain unknown.

## Why he matters to Dylan's story

Control Resonant centers Dylan after years of confinement. Official story material frames his past with the FBC as central to the question of who he is now. A Darling recording about Dylan's arrival can provide institutional context without making the scientist the protagonist.

The first Control used Darling's presentations to explain research and Bureau procedure while gradually revealing his connection to the crisis. Resonant's brief clip proves the presentation format returns, but it does not guarantee the same narrative function or quantity.
