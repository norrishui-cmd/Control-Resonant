## Quick answer

**Crush is a huge, slow, smashing mallet selected from the Secondary Form pool.** It is not one of the three opening basic-attack forms; it is offered later beside Drill and Extend to define a special or charged attack role.

## Secondary Form, not Primary Form

The distinction matters for search and build planning. Flurry, Slash and Slice determine the initial main moveset. Crush, Drill and Extend occupy a separate selection used for secondary attacks. A build can therefore combine a fast or wide Primary Form with Crush's heavier impact instead of choosing one identity for every input.

Xbox Wire uses Crush and Slash together as an example of mixing a heavy mallet with faster attacks. That confirms cross-pool combinations. It does not confirm a named preset, unique bonus or final damage multiplier for that pairing.

## Published strengths and limitations

Crush's two verified characteristics are scale and speed: it is large and slow. Slow attacks usually require more commitment, but the official preview does not publish armor, interruption resistance, stagger power or area size. “Heavy” should not be converted into unverified frame data.

Its likely value is giving a quicker Primary Form access to a deliberate impact tool. Whether it is optimal against bosses, armor or crowds will depend on the final enemy rules and upgrade tree.

## Choosing between the secondaries

The early decision presents three different problems to solve. Crush supplies a powerful smash, Drill applies directed sustained damage, and Extend provides range through a whip-like chain staff. The correct choice depends on what the Primary Form lacks.

Xbox Wire indicates that unselected forms become available later, but the unlock path is not public. Until final balance can be tested, use role coverage instead of a tier list.
