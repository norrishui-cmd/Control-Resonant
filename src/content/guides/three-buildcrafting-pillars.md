## Quick answer

**A CONTROL Resonant build has three central pillars: supernatural Combat Abilities, Aberrant Weapon Forms and passive Talents.** Dylan manages these systems in the Gap while outside combat.

## Pillar one: Combat Abilities

Major Resonant bosses award supernatural powers, sometimes through branching permanent choices. Barrage, Seekers and Shield are three shown examples. Dylan eventually equips three Combat Abilities, expanding active offense, defense, summons and control.

## Pillar two: Weapon Forms

The Aberrant uses a Primary Form, Secondary Form and Combo Ender. Named forms occupy different speed, range and target roles, and individual form trees add alternate specializations and modifier effects.

## Pillar three: Talents

Talents provide passive modifiers and synergy, including branches concerned with status effects. Exclusive choices mean the first playthrough cannot reveal every passive path.

## How they work together

The pillars meet in combat. Melee restores ability resource; abilities can stun; executions temporarily strengthen melee. A Talent can then reinforce a chosen interaction rather than acting as an unrelated bonus. The Gap is the interface and story space where those choices are shaped, not a fourth damage system.
