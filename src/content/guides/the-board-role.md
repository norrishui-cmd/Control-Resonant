## Quick answer

**The Board returns in Control Resonant's opening and addresses Dylan directly when he receives the Aberrant.** The preview confirms its voice and presence, but not whether it created the weapon, controls it or remains an ally.

## Confirmed opening appearance

After Dylan wakes in an open cell, the Aberrant is embedded in his chest. The Board speaks in its familiar layered, contradictory style and frames Dylan as a player-character-like servant. Xbox Wire attributes the weapon's arrival to Jesse and the FBC's governing extradimensional entity in the scene, but the precise chain of ownership is still part of the mystery.

This is evidence of involvement, not proof that the Board occupies the same institutional position it held at the start of the first Control.

## Relationship to Dylan

Dylan was previously considered within the Bureau's director-candidate system. Control Resonant now places him at the center of a crisis outside the Oldest House, carrying a paranatural weapon and searching for Jesse. The Board's command creates an immediate tension between his desire for agency and another powerful institution assigning him a role.

Official story text repeatedly emphasizes Dylan's struggle to decide who he is after years of control by others. That theme makes the Board relevant even before its later objectives are known.

## What remains unresolved

There is no confirmation that the Board is responsible for the new Resonant force, that it can be trusted, or that it serves as the final antagonist. The preview does not explain whether Dylan visits the Astral Plane or receives Hotline-style messages.
