## Quick answer

**CONTROL Resonant is an action-RPG with extensive buildcrafting, but the published preview describes that depth as separated from the repetitive loot grind often associated with the genre.** It is not presented as a loot-shooter.

## Where customization comes from

Build variety comes from three deliberate systems: supernatural abilities earned from Resonant bosses, the Aberrant's weapon forms and passive Talent branches. Artifacts add crafted passive equipment. These are authored progression choices rather than evidence of endlessly randomized weapon drops.

The game's world follows the same philosophy. Remedy describes handcrafted Manhattan zones with encounters, lore, secrets, environmental challenges, hidden discoveries and narrative World Quests. The goal is exploration on the player's terms, not clearing a map full of repetitive markers.

## What the statement does not prove

The preview does not publish the entire reward economy. Enemies may drop resources, activities may award crafting materials and Artifacts may vary; none of that automatically turns the game into a live-service loot loop. Exact rarity systems and post-launch rewards should be documented from the retail build.
