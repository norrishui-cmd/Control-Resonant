## Quick answer

**Drill is a directed, sustained-damage Secondary Form for the Aberrant.** It appears in the same early selection as the slow Crush mallet and ranged Extend chain staff, but the preview does not reveal its final statistics or full input behavior.

## What “sustained” confirms

The Xbox Wire description separates Drill from a single large impact. Its purpose is continued pressure directed at a target. That could suit encounters where Dylan can hold position long enough to maintain contact, but the source does not specify duration, resource use, hit frequency or whether the attack can be redirected after it begins.

Because Drill is a Secondary Form, it complements rather than replaces the main attack sequence. A player could use a group-oriented Primary Form for regular pressure and reserve Drill for a priority target. That is a role-based example, not a tested damage recommendation.

## Risk and positioning

A sustained action can create a different exposure window from a quick strike. Control Resonant's fights include enemies attacking at both melee and range, so the real value of Drill will depend on cancellation rules, movement freedom and interruption behavior. None of those details has been finalized in public documentation.

Abilities may create safer opportunities. Shield provides defense and an offensive dash, while summons can attack separately. The combat resource loop also pushes Dylan back toward melee after using supernatural powers. Those systems explain possible context without proving a specific combo.

## Unlock and upgrade status

The media build offered Drill as one of three secondary choices after the opening combat. Xbox Wire reports that other weapon forms can be unlocked later and that each weapon has skill-tree customization. The exact Drill nodes, costs and unlock point remain unpublished.
