## Quick answer

**Remedy confirmed that the first CONTROL Resonant gameplay footage shown around State of Play was running on PlayStation 5 Pro.** This demonstrates a native current-generation PlayStation build, but it does not publish the final Pro graphics modes.

## What the footage establishes

The official PlayStation article identifies PS5 Pro as the captured hardware and then explains combat, Manhattan's handcrafted zones and buildcrafting. It is therefore valid evidence for the platform used in that demonstration. It is not a benchmark: edited footage cannot establish locked frame rate, internal resolution, ray tracing, PSSR use or parity with a retail build.

The game also releases on the standard PS5. No official source says PS5 Pro is required, and no content has been announced as exclusive to the enhanced console. Standard and Digital Deluxe purchase differences are edition questions, not hardware-feature differences.

## What to wait for

Useful final comparisons require named quality/performance modes, their resolution targets, frame-rate caps and confirmation of DualSense or accessibility behavior in the shipping build. Those details should be added after a PlayStation store update, Remedy specification or measured retail test.
