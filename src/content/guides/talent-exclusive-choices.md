## Quick answer

**Talent trees in CONTROL Resonant contain exclusive choices, so a first playthrough cannot reveal every passive option.** Talents are designed around synergies—including status effects—rather than only flat upgrades.

## Why the choice matters

The official combat presentation separates Talents from Aberrant weapon forms and supernatural Combat Abilities. Talents provide passive modifiers that support the way those active systems interact. A branch can therefore influence an entire build instead of merely adding an isolated stat.

Xbox Wire reports that exclusive talent choices restrict what can be seen in one run. That makes a branch decision meaningful, but the published material does not provide a respec policy, final tree layout, currency costs or a list of every status effect. A pre-release page should explain the decision model without pretending those missing rules are known.

## How to approach the first run

Start with a combat role: close-range pressure, direct ability damage, defense, crowd control or summons. Then use talents that reinforce that role and its resource loop. Avoid choosing a passive solely because it appears numerically large in preview footage; retail balance and wording can change.
