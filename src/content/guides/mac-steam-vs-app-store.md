## Quick answer

**The Mac version of CONTROL Resonant is planned for both Steam and Apple's App Store later in 2026.** It does not launch with Windows, PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S on September 24.

## Two confirmed Mac storefronts

Remedy's official game page names Steam and the App Store for Mac. That gives Mac players a choice of storefront, but it does not yet establish universal purchase entitlement between them. Buying a Windows Steam copy may expose a Mac build under the same Steam license if the final package is cross-platform; the official wording does not by itself guarantee how licensing will be configured.

An App Store copy is distributed through Apple's ecosystem and should be treated as a separate purchase route unless its listing states otherwise. Save compatibility, achievements, controller support, iCloud behavior and cross-save with Windows or consoles have not been detailed in the official platform announcement.

## Timing and hardware

"Later in 2026" is a release window, not an exact Mac date. The Windows minimum and recommended specifications cannot be translated directly into an Apple-silicon model list. Players should wait for Remedy or the storefronts to publish supported chips, operating-system versions and storage requirements.
