## Quick answer

**Zoe De Vera is Dylan Faden's FBC field handler, and their relationship is built around trust, responsibility and learning to rely on other people.** Remedy describes it as an emotional anchor for CONTROL Resonant.

## Why their perspectives differ

Dylan spent years isolated inside the Oldest House after being held by the Bureau. Zoe survived the catastrophe outside and works in the organization's Manhattan field operation. She therefore understands the active crisis and FBC response, while Dylan brings personal history with the Bureau and extraordinary parautilitarian power.

Their handler relationship can deliver objectives and context, but the official description emphasizes mutual reliance rather than a purely remote mission voice. CONTROL Resonant's seamless dialogue system allows conversations to unfold while the player explores, supporting that relationship without always stopping movement.

## What is confirmed—and what is not

Zoe's professional role and the emotional themes are confirmed. No official source identifies her as a romance option, promises player-controlled relationship outcomes or publishes a list of dialogue choices. Those claims should not be inferred from the word "relationship."
