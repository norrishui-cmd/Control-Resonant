## Quick answer

**CONTROL Resonant includes hidden discoveries, combat encounters, environmental challenges and optional activities inside its Manhattan zones, in addition to the main Dylan's Journey and narrative World Quests.**

## How these activities differ

Dylan's Journey is the main campaign. World Quests are self-contained narrative stories. The other activity types can support exploration without necessarily becoming full story quests. An environmental challenge may test movement or gravity manipulation; a hidden discovery may reward attention to an unusual space; an encounter can emphasize combat; an Altered Item chase can become its own paranormal objective.

This structure matters for guides because those intents should not be collapsed into one generic "side quest" label. A future walkthrough needs the activity's real name, location, requirement, solution and reward before receiving an indexable page.

## What is useful before launch

Players can expect reasons to revisit and examine handcrafted zones rather than simply follow a main objective marker. The official design goal is not to clear a crowded map. That supports a curated activity database after verified play data becomes available, but it does not justify inventing dozens of unnamed collectible or puzzle routes today.
