## Quick answer

**The Evacuation Zone is a named open combat area shown through a later preview save.** The hands-on describes several enemy types attacking from different directions, including both melee and ranged threats, while Dylan has a much fuller set of abilities.

## Why the preview jumped here

Xbox Wire first played the opening hour, where the game introduces choices gradually. It then loaded a later save in the Evacuation Zone to demonstrate the combat system at greater complexity. This makes the area evidence for developed build play, not proof that the whole zone appears early.

The reporter found the complete kit difficult to absorb when received all at once. That supports Remedy's progression approach: Primary, Secondary and finisher forms, Combat Abilities, weapon skill trees and Artifacts are meant to accumulate through play.

## Encounter structure

The Evacuation Zone is described as open enough for enemies to approach from all sides. Melee and ranged units create competing priorities, so simply repeating one attack was not effective in the preview. The report explicitly says later combat could not be solved by button mashing.

No final enemy names, reward table, map boundaries or activity count are supplied. “Open combat area” should not be rewritten as a survival mode or repeatable arena unless Remedy confirms one.

## Build implications

The encounter illustrates why role coverage matters. A group-oriented form can manage pressure, a focused tool can punish a priority target, and abilities can add range, protection or independent summons. Exact best builds remain unknowable without final statistics.
