## Quick answer

**The first boss shown in the opening hands-on is a Resonant Entity shaped like an enormous woman's head, cut off at the mouth and embedded in pavement.** It lifts rocks and street debris telekinetically, and defeating it leads to a choice between Barrage, Seekers and Shield.

## Encounter setup

Dylan reaches the boss after a traversal tutorial combining dash, double jump and levitation. Before the fight, the entity is observed removing Hiss from the surrounding area, indicating that the new Resonant crisis and the escaped Hiss are not simply one unified faction.

The preview describes projectiles made from rocks and garbage. Another hands-on depiction refers to a huge head assembled from urban material. Neither cited source supplies a final proper name, so this page uses the generic “first Resonant Entity” label rather than inventing one.

## Reward choice

After victory, the demo offers three Combat Abilities:

- Barrage, which launches rocks at range;
- Seekers, an autonomous attacker that can be thrown as an explosive;
- Shield, a floating-rock barrier that can ram enemies during a dash.

This demonstrates the broader rule that Resonant bosses expand Dylan's supernatural arsenal and can force meaningful first-run choices.
