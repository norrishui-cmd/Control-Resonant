## Quick answer

**Crowd control in CONTROL Resonant is built around managing threats that approach Dylan from several angles, using weapon coverage, supernatural abilities, summons, movement and stuns rather than relying on a gun line.**

## Why groups are different

The first Control allowed Jesse to keep many fights at range. Dylan's Aberrant places him closer to enemies, so ranged attackers and melee pressure can surround him. The later preview's Evacuation Zone deliberately filled an open arena with both types, and jumping into a fully unlocked save was difficult to manage.

Several confirmed tools solve different pieces of that problem. Slash uses wide scythe swings. Shield protects and can ram through an approach. Barrage reaches a distant threat. Seekers and Mold Turrets create independent pressure. Combat Abilities can stun targets and expose executions.

## A role-based control plan

Identify the enemy that restricts movement, use range or a summon to disrupt it, and avoid committing to a long melee chain while another attacker has a clear angle. An execution can produce a temporary melee buff, but the animation opportunity should be evaluated against the rest of the group.

Final enemy archetypes, invulnerability windows and stagger values require retail testing. This guide therefore maps tool roles rather than prescribing a universal combo.
