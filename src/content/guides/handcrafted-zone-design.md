## Quick answer

**CONTROL Resonant divides Manhattan into major handcrafted zones with distinct atmosphere, encounters, lore and secrets.** They connect through an evolving FBC field-office hub instead of forming one seamless open world.

## Why the zones are separate

Paranatural forces affect each part of the city differently. Remedy uses that division to create stronger visual and mechanical variety than a single continuous space could provide. The structure also avoids a map designed around repetitive clearing or excessive markers.

The known FBC field office acts as a central area from which major zones branch. West Incursion Zone is an identified early area, and Evacuation Zone appeared as an open combat arena in the media build. Those examples prove more than one environment type, but they do not reveal the final zone count.

## Exploration inside a zone

Each area can contain campaign objectives, World Quests, combat encounters, environmental challenges, hidden discoveries, lore, secrets and optional activities. "Handcrafted" describes authored density and identity; it does not mean every zone is linear or that backtracking is absent.
