## Quick answer

**The Federal Bureau of Control operates a field office that serves as an evolving hub for Manhattan's major zones.** Xbox Wire describes the zones as branching from this central area rather than forming one completely seamless open world.

## World structure

Control Resonant expands beyond the Oldest House but keeps authored area boundaries. The preview says Manhattan is divided into major zones connected through the FBC field office. Each zone has a different atmosphere and challenge because the paranatural crisis affects it differently.

This structure helps reconcile two official descriptions: the game is much larger and more open than the first Control, yet Remedy explicitly says it is not a conventional open-world checklist. The hub-and-zones model supports exploration without requiring every street to be one uninterrupted map.

## Why the hub evolves

Xbox Wire calls the field office an evolving hub. That wording suggests its state changes as the campaign progresses, but the source does not specify facilities, vendors, upgrade stations, NPC schedules or visual stages. Those details should not be manufactured from genre conventions.

Remedy separately confirms that the FBC remains active in the field while many civilians have evacuated. Zoe De Vera is an FBC field agent and Dylan's handler, providing a human link to the Bureau's response.

## What remains unknown

There is no official floor plan, fast-travel menu or list of services for the field office. It is also not confirmed as the only route between every zone. Launch evidence is required before producing room-by-room pages.
