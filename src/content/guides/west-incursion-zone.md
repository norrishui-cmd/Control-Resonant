## Quick answer

**The West Incursion Zone is a confirmed early-game area in warped Manhattan.** Remedy used it to demonstrate the hunt for a Resonant, large handcrafted zones, optional discoveries and architecture that grows more distorted as gravity becomes unreliable.

## Place in the campaign

Official gameplay footage follows Dylan through the West Incursion Zone while he hunts a Resonant. Remedy calls it one of the areas available early in the game, not the name of the entire Manhattan map or a standalone expansion.

The zone starts from a more recognizable urban frame and becomes increasingly altered. Architecture folds, orientation changes and Gravity Anomalies turn surfaces into unexpected paths. Zoe De Vera provides field guidance during this material.

## What can be found there

Remedy says Control Resonant's zones contain side activities, hidden encounters and optional discoveries around a central narrative. That tells us the West Incursion Zone is more than a corridor, but it does not reveal a final checklist, collectible total or exact quest names.

The studio also distinguishes the game from a traditional open world. Zones are large and expansive, yet carefully designed with their own encounters, lore and secrets. The goal is exploration rather than clearing a map full of repetitive icons.
