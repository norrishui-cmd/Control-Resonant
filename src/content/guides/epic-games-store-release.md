## Quick answer

**CONTROL Resonant launches on the Epic Games Store on September 24, 2026, the same day as the Steam and console versions.** The official platform list rules out a timed Steam-only or Epic-only Windows launch.

## What is confirmed

Epic is one of the two named native Windows storefronts. The game is developed and published by Remedy Entertainment, while Annapurna Pictures co-finances and co-produces it. Epic store availability does not change that publishing arrangement.

The release date, Windows platform and core game are confirmed. Store-specific achievements, cloud saves, overlay behavior, offline authentication and cross-store save transfer should be evaluated from the final Epic listing rather than inferred from Steam features. Steam's Family Sharing and controller labels apply to Steam; they do not automatically certify an equivalent Epic feature.

## Epic or Steam?

The correct choice depends on the ecosystem you already use. Steam currently exposes the full PC requirements and named store features. Epic gives players another purchase route without waiting for a later port. Neither storefront has been announced as receiving exclusive story missions, bosses or combat abilities.

Edition bonuses can vary by platform and store presentation. Compare the actual listing before purchase instead of treating one store's bundle text as universal.
