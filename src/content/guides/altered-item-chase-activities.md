## Quick answer

**Altered Item chases are teased as one type of optional activity inside CONTROL Resonant's Manhattan zones.** Remedy has not released an item list, exact chase count, locations or rewards.

## Why "chase" is meaningful

The official world-design article places Altered Items beside encounters, environmental challenges, hidden discoveries and other optional activities. The phrasing suggests an active discovery or pursuit rather than only reading a static collectible document. It also preserves a recognizable part of the Control universe while moving exploration into Manhattan.

That evidence supports an activity overview, but not individual item pages. A useful item guide needs a verified name, starting condition, exact zone, route or puzzle, containment outcome and reward. Generating those fields before gameplay evidence would create placeholders.

## How this cluster will expand

After reliable hands-on or review access, each distinct Altered Item can receive one canonical page when it answers a real location or solution query. A hub can then organize chases by zone and prerequisite. Duplicate pages for an item's name, location and reward should be avoided unless those searches require materially different answers.
