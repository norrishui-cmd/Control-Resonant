## Quick answer

**Seekers supports a tactical summon build: deploy the auto-firing entity for independent pressure, then throw or detonate it as an explosive when repositioning or finishing a group.** It was one of three permanent first-boss choices in the media demo.

## Why the summon changes combat

An autonomous attacker divides attention. While Seekers fires, Dylan can close distance, restore ability resource through melee, manage another enemy or prepare an execution. The ability is not passive-only because its throwable explosive function gives the player an active conversion point.

That dual role distinguishes Seekers from Barrage's direct projectile and Shield's defense/ram behavior. It also suggests a different rhythm: deploy for area pressure, act elsewhere, then convert the summon when the battlefield changes.

## Confirmed build framework

Pair the summon with an Aberrant form that can quickly exploit distracted or grouped enemies. Use movement to keep Dylan out of crossfire while the summon operates. A Talent branch may eventually strengthen status, summon or execution interactions, but no official source has published a complete Seeker tree or final damage values.
