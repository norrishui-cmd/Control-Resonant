## Quick answer

**Status effects are a confirmed part of CONTROL Resonant build synergy: passive Talents can be organized around effects applied to enemies, and Mold Turrets can independently apply harmful effects.** The complete effect list and stacking rules remain unreleased.

## Confirmed interaction points

The game supports more than direct weapon damage. A tactical summon can maintain pressure and debuff targets while Dylan moves, attacks or prepares an execution. Talents then create passive links between abilities and melee actions. This is the foundation for a status-oriented build even though names, durations and percentages are not yet public.

The distinction between an effect source and an effect payoff is useful. Mold Turrets are a known source. A Talent may reward applying or exploiting an effect. The Aberrant form and chosen Combat Ability determine how Dylan converts that opening into damage, safety or crowd control.

## Building without invented numbers

Before launch, select complementary roles rather than claiming a finished meta. A summon can cover space; Shield can protect an approach; Barrage can reach a distant target; fast melee can restore ability resource. Whether a particular effect stacks, refreshes, spreads or affects bosses requires final-game testing.

Read the [Mold Turret guide](/guides/mold-turrets/) for its confirmed independent behavior and the [Talent choice guide](/guides/talent-exclusive-choices/) for permanent branches. The [combat momentum guide](/guides/combat-momentum-and-executions/) explains the separate stun-and-execution loop. These systems may cooperate, but a status condition should not be mislabeled as the same thing as an execution stun.
