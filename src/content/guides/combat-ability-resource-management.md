## Quick answer

**Dylan restores his Combat Ability resource by landing melee hits.** Spending that resource on supernatural powers can stun enemies, creating executions that temporarily increase melee damage and help sustain the aggressive loop.

## The resource cycle

The sequence is intentionally circular rather than a one-way mana bar. Aberrant attacks refill the resource. Combat Abilities convert it into offense, defense, summons or control. A stunned enemy may become executable. Completing the execution strengthens subsequent melee damage for a limited period, rewarding the player for closing distance again.

This design explains why Barrage does not turn the game into a permanent ranged caster. It also explains why Shield and summons remain connected to melee: their tactical value helps Dylan stay active, but the resource supply still comes from close combat.

## Practical planning before final numbers

Do not empty every ability charge without a route back into melee. Use movement and control to create a safe approach, choose an Aberrant form suited to the target group, then spend resource where it can create the next opening. Exact regeneration per hit, execution thresholds and buff duration require the retail build.
