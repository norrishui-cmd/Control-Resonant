## Quick answer

**CONTROL Resonant is confirmed for GeForce NOW when the Windows version launches on September 24, 2026.** This is a cloud-streaming option, not a separate edition of the game and not a replacement for buying it from a supported PC storefront.

## What the confirmation means

Remedy lists GeForce NOW alongside the September launch platforms. That confirms planned service availability, while Windows PC receives native releases through Steam and the Epic Games Store. Mac follows later in 2026 through Steam and the App Store.

GeForce NOW normally streams a supported copy associated with a participating storefront account. Remedy has not published the final storefront-link matrix or service-tier performance targets for CONTROL Resonant, so this page does not assume that every edition, region or store will be available in the cloud on identical terms.

## Who should consider cloud play

Cloud streaming may be useful if a local PC falls below the listed GTX 1070/RX 5600 XT minimum graphics tier or lacks the 100 GB installation space. It does not erase connection requirements: latency, bandwidth, queue times, resolution and session length depend on NVIDIA's service and the player's membership tier.

This option is also different from [Xbox Play Anywhere](/guides/xbox-play-anywhere/), which covers ownership across Xbox console and Xbox on PC. For native hardware details, use the [PC requirements table](/guides/system-requirements/). The [platform hub](/guides/platforms-and-performance/) keeps native, cloud and later Mac availability separate so one label is not mistaken for another.
