# Multilingual Core Rollout V14

## Scope

V14 deepens the seven official-language sections that previously contained only
a localized home and language-support page:

- Korean
- Spanish — Latin America
- Traditional Chinese
- Polish
- Russian
- Turkish
- Ukrainian

Each locale receives three evidence-backed detail pages and a Guides hub:
release/editions, platforms/system requirements, and combat/buildcrafting.

## Quality rules

- Every detail route maps to one established English search intent.
- Pages reuse the English detail component and module order.
- Each page includes a direct answer, multi-section body, official sources,
  related guides, five News links, and at least five FAQ links.
- Korean and Traditional Chinese use a 500-character content-density gate.
- Latin and Cyrillic locales use a 220-visible-word gate.
- Hreflang connects all 15 official languages where equivalent pages exist.
- No unannounced platform, performance target, combat number, cooldown, drop
  rate, or final build ranking is presented as fact.
