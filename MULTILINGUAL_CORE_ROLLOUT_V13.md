# Multilingual core-guide rollout — V13

## Result

- Verified sitemap: 322 → 347 canonical URLs.
- Added 20 localized core guides: four each in Italian, Spanish (Spain), Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), and Simplified Chinese.
- Added five localized Guides hubs, one for each expanded language.
- Preserved all 15 official language home and language-support routes.
- Preserved 101 English guides, 49 German details, 49 French details, 35 News reports, 50 FAQ pages, the database, AdSense, and `ads.txt`.

## New core clusters

Each expanded locale now has a native-language page for:

1. Release date, editions, pre-order items, and confirmed timing.
2. Platforms, PC requirements, Mac/cloud status, and performance limits.
3. Combat, Aberrant, abilities, executions, The Gap, Talents, Artifacts, and honest build boundaries.
4. Dylan, Jesse, Zoe, FBC, Manhattan, and the spoiler-safe story premise.

## English-style parity

The new pages share the English detail-page renderer and therefore use the same:

- breadcrumb and hero hierarchy;
- verification stamp and direct-answer panel;
- sectioned article body;
- visible official-source card;
- related-guide grid;
- five related News and at least five related FAQ links;
- desktop/mobile navigation, language dropdown, responsive spacing, and AdSense inheritance.

Each new language also has a CollectionPage/ItemList Guides hub using the English hub layout.

## SEO and quality gates

- One search intent and self-referencing canonical per URL.
- EN/DE/FR/IT/ES/JA/PT-BR/ZH-CN plus `x-default` hreflang for matching core topics.
- Word-density checks for Latin-script pages and CJK-character checks for Japanese and Simplified Chinese.
- No unsupported damage values, frame-rate claims, Mac date, Steam Deck rating, or unannounced platform pages.
- Complete build, internal-link, title, description, H1, schema, News/FAQ module, AdSense, and `ads.txt` audit passed.
