# German and Japanese Expansion V16

## Scope

V16 adds 16 evidence-backed localized detail URLs:

- 8 German pages covering combat-resource flow, action-RPG structure,
  3D platforming, Reach, Shift, PS5 Pro evidence, first-run build locks,
  and Mold Turret/status builds.
- 8 Japanese pages covering New Game Plus, Aberrant forms, movement,
  world structure, Dylan, Jesse, Zoe, and World Quests.

German grows from 49 to 57 detail pages. Japanese grows from 4 to 12 detail
pages. Existing English, French, FAQ, News, database, and all 15 official
language routes remain intact.

## Parity and quality rules

- All new routes use the same English detail component and responsive layout.
- Every page contains breadcrumb, verification stamp, direct answer, structured
  sections, official sources, related guides, five News links, and at least
  five relevant FAQ links.
- Every route maps to one existing English search intent and receives reciprocal
  hreflang where an equivalent translation exists.
- German pages pass the 220-visible-word localized-content gate.
- Japanese pages pass the CJK character-density and metadata gates.
- No final damage values, cooldowns, drops, quest steps, map coordinates,
  performance modes, or story outcomes are invented before release.
